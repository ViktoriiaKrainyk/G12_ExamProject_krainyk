#01 - Test for valid login
Steps:
1. Open HomePage
2. Check the redirection to HomePage
3. Check the presence of LoginToAccount button
4. Check the text of the LoginToAccount button
5. Check the count of Favorites on the button is not displayed for non logged-in users
6. Click on the LoginToAccount button
7. Check the Authorization form
8. Enter valid login and password
9. Click on the Sign In button
10. Check the Authorization form is closed
11. Check the redirection to HomePage
12. Check the Account button is displayed
13. Check the count of Favorites on the Account button is displayed

#02 - Test for updating contact information
Preconditions:
1. User is logged in with valid credentials
2. Update the contact information fields with valid data and save

Steps:
1. Open HomePage
2. Open Authorization form and enter valid login and password
3. Click on the Sign In button
4. Check the redirection to HomePage
5. Click on the Account button
6. Check is Personal Account page is opened
7. Check the presence tabs on the Personal Account page
8. Check that the Contact Information tab is active
9. Check initial values of contact information fields (from preconditions)
10. Update the contact information fields with valid data and save
11. Refresh the page
12. Check that the updated contact information is displayed correctly

Postconditions:
1. User is logged in with valid credentials if not already logged in
2. Update the contact information fields with valid data (from preconditions)

#04 - Test for adding a product to Favorites
Preconditions:
1. User is logged in with valid credentials
2. Check the count of Favorites on the button
3. Delete all products from Favorites if any exist
4. Log out

Steps:
1. Open HomePage
2. Open Authorization form and enter valid login and password
3. Click on the Sign In button
4. Check the redirection to HomePage
5. Check the count of Favorites on the button is 0
6. Open Favorites page and check the Favorites page is empty
7. Navigate to a Parfums page
8. Check the redirection to Parfums page
9. Check the presence of products on the Parfums page
10. Select a product and check the product page is opened
11. Click on the Add to Favorites button
12. Click on MonitorPriceChangesButton: (yes)
13. Check the count of Favorites on the button is updated to 1
14. Open Favorites page and check the product is displayed in Favorites
15. Check the MonitorPriceChanges checkbox is checked for the added product

Postconditions:
1. User is logged in with valid credentials if not already logged in
2. Check the count of Favorites on the button
3. Delete all products from Favorites if any exist

#05 - Test for removing added product from Favorites
Preconditions:
1. User is logged in with valid credentials
2. Open HomePage
3. Cleenup the favorites to contain no more than 5 products
4. Save productIds of the products in Favorites
5. Click on the Add to Favorites button
6. Click on MonitorPriceChangesButton: (no)
7. Check the count of Favorites on the button is updated
8. Log out

Steps:
1. Open HomePage
2. Open Authorization form and enter valid login and password
3. Click on the Sign In button
4. Check the redirection to HomePage
5. Check the count of Favorites on the button equals to the count after adding a new product in the preconditions
6. Open Favorites page and check the redirection to Favorites page
7. Check the presence of added product from preconditions in Favorites
8. Check the MonitorPriceChanges checkbox is not checked for the added product
9. Click on the Remove from Favorites button for the added product from preconditions
10. Check the product is removed from Favorites
11. Refresh the page
12. Check the count of Favorites on the button is decreased by 1
